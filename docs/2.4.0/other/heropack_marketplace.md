The marketplace is an in-game online way to browse, install, and update heropacks.

### Using the marketplace

To access the marketplace open the `Select HeroPacks` menu through the escape menu. Then at the bottom left you will see a letter tray icon that opens the marketplace. Through this menu you can install, uninstall, or update heropacks, view their gallery or CurseForge pages as well as information about the pack. To install a heropack from the marketplace on a server, you have to use commands:\n`/heropacks marketplace query` to see what packs exist on the marketplace\n`/heropacks marketplace query <domain>` to get more info on a specific pack\n`/heropacks marketplace install <domain>` to install a pack\n`/heropacks marketplace uninstall <domain>` to uninstall a pack\nOnce you've installed/uninstalled all the packs you want, you then have to run `/fiskheroes reload --resources`. If the pack you are trying to install has a `LICENSE.txt` you will need to agree with the license before installing the pack.

### Getting on the marketplace

This is intended to make it as easy as possible for people to find and use your packs, as well as growing the heropacking community. If you have a pack of your own, I'm sure you've been wondering if and how you can get your own pack on there, and I have good news - the marketplace is open to everyone! In order to promote innovation, creativity, and quality within the heropacking community, the HeroPack Marketplace should be open for everyone, regardless of clout or status. This means that, in the interest of fairness, everyone has to go through the same process. To ensure the quality (and safety) of all the content on the marketplace, there is a set of guidelines that every pack must adhere to in order to qualify. If your pack is accepted, it will immediately be added to the marketplace, ready for everyone to download and use with just a single click. However, if it's rejected, you will still have the chance to apply again in the future once your pack qualifies.
